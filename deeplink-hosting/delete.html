Delete Your Account & Data

Dot Clash AI — Last updated: 16 July 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of your account and associated data in Dot Clash AI (developer: PixelPlay).

Option 1 — Delete instantly inside the app (recommended)

  1. Open Dot Clash AI.
  2. Go to the Profile tab (bottom bar).
  3. Scroll down and tap "Delete Account & Data".
  4. Confirm on the dialog. Your account and data are deleted immediately.

Option 2 — Request by email

  1. Send an email to sandeep.jan19@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject line "Delete my data".
  3. Send it from the email address linked to your Google sign-in, or include your in-game display name so we can locate your data.

We confirm and complete the deletion within 30 days.

What data is deleted

On request, we permanently delete:

DataAction
Firebase authentication record (Google account UID, email)Deleted
Display nameDeleted
Leaderboard entries (name, score)Deleted
Online match data (room codes, moves)Cleared automatically after each match

What data is kept

  • Coins, XP, and game progress are stored only on your device (not on our servers). Uninstalling the App removes them — nothing is kept by us.
  • Anonymous analytics and crash logs (Firebase Analytics / Crashlytics) are pseudonymous, not linked to your identity, and are retained per Firebase defaults (up to 14 months) before being automatically deleted.
